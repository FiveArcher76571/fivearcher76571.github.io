Meet the Performers

Geeta's journey is a remarkable blend of her love for the classical arts and her medical aspirations. Her passion for the Veena, nurtured by a distinguished guru, Vidwan Sri Prashanth Rudrapatna Prasannakumar, shines in her performances, and it's amazing to see her actively bringing Indian classical music to a wider audience. Playing in front of Governor Gretchen Whitmer is quite an achievement!
Balancing a deep involvement in music while pursuing a career in medicine at MSU speaks to her determination and versatility. It's wonderful to know she's using her talent to enrich her community through events like the Margazhi festival, Great Lakes Aradhana Committee, Mai Family Services fundraisers, and many more. A rare mix of artistic depth and academic ambition, Geeta's story is one worth celebrating.
