Kumari Geeta Gayathri Vemuri

Veena

Geeta's journey is a remarkable blend of her love for the classical arts and her medical aspirations. Her passion for carnatic music led her to develop an interest in the Veena at 10 years old. She is nurtured by a distinguished guru, Vidwan Sri Prashanth Rudrapatna Prasannakumar, and it is amazing to see her actively bringing Indian classical music to a wider audience.

Balancing a deep involvement in music while pursuing a career in medicine at MSU speaks to her determination and versatility. It's wonderful to know she's using her talent to enrich her community through events like the Margazhi festival, Great Lakes Aradhana Committee, Mai Family Services fundraisers, and many more. She displays a rare mix of artistic depth and academic ambition.

Concerts and Performances

Geeta has spread the joy of music on several stages such as:

  • Margazhi Festival at the SV Temple
  • GLAC in Flint
  • Mai Family Services Fundraisers
  • SVBF Navaratri festivals
  • Solo Performance at the Parashakti Temple
  • Performances with the Veena group Panchaveene

Academic Excellence

Along with her love of music, Geeta excelled in her academics with her hard work and perseverance. Below is a sneak peak of her academic achievements:

  • Top 8 in HOSA States Leadership Conference (competitive medical club)
  • FHS JV Tennis for 4 years
  • PTSA Secretary for 3 years
  • Quiz Bowl Varsity C team captain
  • BLS Certified
  • Received the Youth Recognition Award
  • Received the Certificate of Recognition for Volunteerism
  • Received the Oakland County Executive David Coulter Special Award of Recognition


Apart from a successful school journey, Geeta is also a part of the Sathya Sai Organization where she attended SSE, Sai Spiritual Education from the age of four. Through SSE, Geeta learned what it meant to stay determined, to persevere, to use discrimination and many more life values. She truly believes that all the achievements she earned so far and will be earning in the future is because of the guidance and love of her spiritual Guru Sri Sathya Sai Baba.
