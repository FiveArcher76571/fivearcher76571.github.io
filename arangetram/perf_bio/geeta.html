Kumari Geeta Gayathri Vemuri

Veena

Throughout her life, Geeta has culminated a passion for the classical arts. She began her musical journey at the age of four with guru Srimathi Vani Harish Rao, whose foundations have shaped her musical knowledge and allowed her to flourish. Her veena guru, Vidwan Sri Prashanth Rudrapatna Prasannakumar, has further brought out the best in her, both musically and personally, and she continues to thrive under his guidance. To this day, she continues to learn and apply the subtle nuances that make music so beautiful.

Concerts and Performances

Geeta spreads the joy of music through several performances, including:

  • Margazhi Festival at the SV Temple
  • GLAC in Flint
  • Mai Family Services Fundraisers
  • SVBF Navaratri festivals
  • Performance at the Parashakti Temple
  • Performances with the Veena group Panchaveene

Academic Excellence

Alongside her love of music, Geeta continues to dedicate herself to her academic pursuits. With her passion in pursuing the medical field, she aims to major in Biomedical Laboratory Science with a minor in Law, Justice, and Public Policy at Michigan State University’s College of Natural Science. She aspires to become a practicing veterinarian and continues to explore several career paths to broaden her professional development.
Her merits include:

  • Top 8 in HOSA States Leadership Conference
  • FHS JV Tennis for 4 years
  • PTSA Secretary for 3 years
  • Quiz Bowl Varsity C team captain
  • BLS Certified
  • Received the Youth Recognition Award
  • Received the Oakland County Executive David Coulter Special Award of Recognition
  • Mehas Family Endowed Scholarship

SSSIO

Along with her academic and musical aspirations, Geeta is a member of the Sri Sathya Sai International Organization (SSSIO), a global non-profit dedicated to preaching values such as truth, right conduct, peace, love, and non-violence. This organization frequently participates in humanitarian, educational, and spiritual activities, and it urges everyone to introspect on who they are as a person. Since she was 5, she received a spiritual education from Sai Spiritual Education (SSE) from Detroit Metro Sai Center, and graduated on June 1st of 2025. Moreover, Geeta serves as the SSSIO Region 5 Student Council president, planning and overseeing service activities across ten states. She continues to be an active member of SSE, and hopes to continue her participation in college.
