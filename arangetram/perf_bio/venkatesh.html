Vidwan Sri Venkatesh Venkataraman

Mridangam

Venkatesh started learning mridangam from Guru Sri V. Sundara Raghavan, primary disciple of Kumbakonam Guru Sri Rajappaiyer.
Venkatesh has accompanied many artists in India, and after he moved to Metro Detroit, he has performed in various concerts accompanying accomplished artists such Asthana Vidwan of Kanchipuram Sri Kamakoti Peetam Rudrapatna Sri Prasanna Kumar and Rudrapatna Brothers Sri Prashanth and Sri Pramod. He always encourages younger artists and enjoys accompanying and giving valuable tips to improve their performance. Venkatesh has also accompanied the students of Sri Prashanth, Srimathi Kasthuri Shivakumar and Sri Jayshankar Balan. Venkatesh also teaches mridangam to students in Troy, MI.
Besides music, Venkatesh loves to play tennis and enjoys watching tennis tournaments during his free time.
