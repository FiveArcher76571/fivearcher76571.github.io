Vidwan Sri Venkatesh Venkataraman

Mridangam

Venkatesh started to learn mridangam from Guru Sri V.Sundara Raghavan, primary disciple of Kumbakonam Guru Sri Rajappaiyer. Venkatesh has accompanied many artists in India and continued his passion for the art of mridangam after moving to the Metro Detroit area.
