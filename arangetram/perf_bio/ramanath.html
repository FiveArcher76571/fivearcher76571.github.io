Vidwan Sri Ramanath Pai

Tabla

Ramanath Pai H, hails from Ajekar, Karnataka, India, and currently resides in Kitchener with his family. During his high school years, he began learning tabla under the guidance of Guru Sri Krishnanand Kamath and secured 1st Rank in the Karnataka Secondary Education Board's Junior Tabla Examination in 2004.


Over the next 14 years, his tabla journey evolved through regular performances at temple bhajan events and accompaniment in diverse local musical forms such as Hari-Katha Sangath, Bharatanatyam, Qawwali, Sugam Sangeet, and Bhavageethe.


In 2018, he advanced his tabla education under Guru Sri Hemant Babtiwale in Farmington Hills, Detroit, USA, and completed the Madhyama Poorna level under ABGM Mandal, Mumbai. During his time in the U.S., he had the opportunity to accompany a variety of musical genres including Kathak dance, Ghazal, Chhota Khayal, Bada Khayal, and Bhavageethe. He also became actively involved with local music bands and community-based events, including performances for Pampa Kannada Koota.


He had the honor of performing alongside Sunitha Ananthaswamy at the Women of Color STEM Conference at the COBO Hall in Detroit, and with Rudrapatna Prasanna Kumar for veena concerts at multiple temple events in the Detroit area. One of the most memorable highlights of his journey was performing tabla with his guru, Shri Hemant Babtiwale, at the Aradhana Concert Series held at the Bharatiya Temple, Troy.


Since 2021, he has continued his tabla journey in Canada, contributing to several classical and semi-classical bhajan groups. Notably, he also had the opportunity to perform Ghazal and Thumri music at an event organized by the Guelph Museum.
