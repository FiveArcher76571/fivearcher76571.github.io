Meet the Guru

Vidwan Sri Prashanth Rudrapatna Prasanna Kumar

Veena, Vocal

Vidwan Sri Prashanth is a singer and Veena player. He has given many concerts in esteemed gatherings like Great Lakes Aradhana Samiti (GLAC)-MI, MI India, India League of America- Michigan, AKKA 2018 Texas, Pampa Kannada Koota-Michigan, Ford Asian Indian Association (FAIA). Visits, dance programs, temples, private and public events and so on.

He has trained many students in Carnatic singing and Veena. He and his younger brother R.P. Pramod, renowned as the Rudrapatna Veena Brothers, hail from the illustrious lineage of musicians from Rudrapatna. Sons of Vidwan Sri R.K. Prasanna Kumar, a Kanchi Kamakoti Peetam Asthana Vidwan and Suvarna Karnataka Chetana Awardee, the brothers inherit a rich tradition of Carnatic music. Their grandfather, Vainika Praveena Sri R.S. Keshava Murthy, was a revered Asthana Vidwan of the Mysore Palace, renowned for his mastery of the Veena.

Musical Heritage and Training

Rooted in a multi-generational legacy, the brothers were immersed in music from a young age, beginning their Veena training at the tender ages of 3 and 7. Their initial tutelage came under their parents, Smt. Poornima and Vidwan R.K. Prasanna Kumar, continuing an unbroken tradition of musical excellence. They further refined their artistry under esteemed gurus, including:

  • Vidwan Sri Srinivasa Murthaachar
  • Vidwan Sri Subanna
  • Kalaimamani Sri R.K. Suryanarayana (their paternal uncle)
  • Kalaimamani Dr. Smt. Jayanthi Kumaresh (Vid. Prashanth's Guru)

Concerts and Performances

The Rudrapatna Veena Brothers have graced prestigious platforms across India, the United States, and the United Kingdom, winning admiration for their unique approach to Veena performance. Their concerts include:

  • All India Radio and Karnataka Culture Department's “Yuva Sourabha”
  • Various Sabhas, TV Channels, and public/private performances in India
  • Cleveland Thyagaraja Aradhana Festival, USA, a revered stage for Carnatic musicians
  • Esteemed venues across North America and the UK, including Great Lakes Aradhana Committee (GLAC), Pampa Kannada Koota, Kannada Koota Scotland, ITC Fine Arts, and Art of Living in the presence of Sri Sri Ravishankar Guruji
  • Performances accompanying dance arangetrams, temple festivals, and cultural celebrations worldwide

Awards and Recognitions

The brothers' artistry and dedication have earned them several prestigious honors:

  • "Navodaya Award" - Conferred by Shubhodaya Trust for their contributions to Carnatic music
  • "Yuva Rudra Gaana Prathibhamani" (2025) - Bestowed at Rudrapatna by the Rudrapatna Sangeetotsava Trust, recognizing their exceptional talent and commitment to preserving the Rudrapatna musical legacy

Professional and Artistic Contributions

Beyond his musical expertise Vid. Prashanth is an accomplished engineering graduate. He works as a Senior Technical Architect at Elektrobit India Pvt. Ltd., balancing his expertise in technology with his passion for music. He is also an integral member of the fusion band “Hrudhwani” in the USA, blending Carnatic traditions with contemporary expressions.

Both brothers have actively trained students in Carnatic vocal and Veena, fostering the next generation of musicians in India, the USA, and the UK. They are also passive contributors to Gurushree Art Center, Bangalore, an initiative nurturing emerging talent.
